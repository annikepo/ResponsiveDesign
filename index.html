Creamy Pumpkin Soup

A rich, comforting classic topped with feta cheese and seeds.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pumpkin (peeled & diced)
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 3 cups vegetable stock
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • Feta cheese & pumpkin seeds

Instructions

  1. Sauté onion and garlic in oil until translucent.
  2. Add pumpkin and stock. Simmer for 20 minutes until tender.
  3. Blend until smooth, then stir in cream.
  4. Serve hot with crumbled feta and toasted pumpkin seeds!
